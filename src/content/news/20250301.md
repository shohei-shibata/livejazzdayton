We finally have a non-profit for jazz music in Dayton! 

Jazz Forward Dayton will bring more live jazz to Dayton, as well as support our community through education and engagement. 

The launch celebration takes place on September 27th and you do not want to miss it. 

[Find out more and get your tickets here](https://livejazzdayton.com/events/2026-9-27-jazz-forward-dayton-launch-celebration/)!
